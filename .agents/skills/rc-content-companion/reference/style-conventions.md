# Style Conventions

## Capitalization

### Sentence case everywhere, except:
- Legal terms: Service Level Agreement, Terms and Conditions
- Admin Portal role names: Super Admin, Billing Admin, Manager
- Proper names: locations, people's first and last names

### ALL CAPS only for:
- Abbreviations and acronyms
- Live status indicators needing visual prominence (e.g., REC)

## Punctuation

### Ampersand (&)
Avoid when possible. Exceptions:
- Titles/subheads/menu items when no room for "and"
- "Plans & Pricing"
- Initialisms (R&D)
- Trademarked or product names

### Period
- Use at the end of all complete sentences (subject + predicate)
- Sentence fragments don't need periods unless followed by another sentence
- No end punctuation in: headers, buttons, links, ghost text, field labels, simple confirmations (3 words or less)

Examples:
```
Correct: The event room is currently full.
Correct: No network connection [no period — fragment]
Correct: Changes saved [no period — simple confirmation]
```

### Quotation Marks
Don't use quotation marks for product names, settings, or features in UI. Use capitalization instead.
```
Correct: You'll see the Network is down error message.
Incorrect: You'll see the "Network is down" error message.
```

### Slash
No spaces on either side.
```
Correct: 24/7
Incorrect: 24 / 7, 24x7
```

### Comma
- Oxford comma: always use when listing 3+ items before "and" or "or"
- Vocative comma in greetings: "Welcome, Anne!"
- Comma before coordinating conjunction (and, but, yet, so, or, nor, for) joining two independent clauses

### Hyphen

**Compound nouns and adjectives** — generally one word, no hyphen:
- teammate, keyboard, keypad, dialpad, deskphone
- backup, dropdown, setup, plugin, popup, popout

**Hyphenate when** the compound could be misread:
- check-in, dial-in/dialed in, sign-in/signed in, single sign-on, add-on, re-collect

**Compound adjectives before a noun** — hyphenate:
- best-of-breed platform, toll-free number, two-factor authentication, three-way conference
- real-time communications (but: "communicating in real time")
- after-hours phone numbers (but: "work done after hours")

**Adverb ending in "ly" + adjective** — never hyphenate:
```
Correct: Newly updated app
Incorrect: Newly-updated app
```

**Phrasal verbs** (verb + preposition) — always two words:
```
Correct: Back up your data / Set up your account / Sign in to your account
Incorrect: Backup your data / Setup your account / Signin to your account
```

## Number Formatting

### General
- Product writing: always use numerals (3 easy steps) unless standard convention (three-way call, two-factor authentication)
- User education: numerals for 10+, spell out one through nine
- Commas in numbers 1,000 and above

### Dates
Full format: Monday, August 3, 2021

Abbreviated (space-constrained):
- Months (no period): Jan Feb Mar Apr May Jun Jul Aug Sep Oct Nov Dec
- Days (no period): Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat
- Year in YYYY for full format, YY for abbreviated: Oct 3, 2021 or Mon, 8/3/21
- US abbreviated: dd/mm/yy (e.g., 8/3/21)
- No leading zeros on single-digit days or months
- Don't mix full and abbreviated in the same date
- Don't use ISO format (year first)

### Times
US format:
- 3:45 PM (no extra 0, no commas)
- AM/PM: all caps, no periods, space before
- Replace 12 PM with "noon" (not midday). Don't replace 12 AM with midnight.
- Replace today's date with "Today": Today, 10 AM PST

Ranges — en dash, no spaces:
- 8 AM–12:30 PM
- Jan 6–Feb 2

Date + time with commas: Monday, April 3, 2021, 10 AM PST

Temporal abbreviations (only when space-constrained):
- hours = hr, minutes = min, seconds = sec (no periods)
- Duration format: 1 hr : 15 min : 20 sec

24/7 coverage: use "24/7" or "24 hours, 7 days a week." Not 24x7 or 24 by 7.

### Phone Numbers
US format:
- (555) 555-5555 (3-3-4 grouping, parentheses around area code)
- Space after parenthesis
- Hyphen between second and third groups, no spaces around hyphen
- US country code: +1 (415) 123-4567
- Extensions: comma + ext. → +1 (415) 123-4567, ext.123

### File Formats
- All caps: PDF, XLS, DOCX, JPEG, WAV, TIFF, EXE, MP3
- No periods or commas
- Don't add "file" after the format

```
Correct: Upload your PDF
Incorrect: Upload your PDF file
```

## Text Styles
Don't use bold to set off textual elements in product UI (except in KB articles). Use capitalization instead.
```
Example: Select from the following, then click Continue.
```
